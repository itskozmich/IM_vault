# 🎭 Character Portrait Prompt Template

**Base prompt:**
A traditional oil painting portrait in the grimdark Warhammer 40,000 style. Rendered in the manner of an old master, with textured brushstrokes and a muted, earthy color palette. The subject stands against a dark, shadowy background, with harsh chiaroscuro lighting that emphasizes wear, grit, and emotional depth. No fantastical elements, no photorealism – everything grounded and painterly.

**Template:**
Subject is a {{age}}-year-old {{gender}}, with {{face_shape}} face, {{hair_color}} {{hairstyle}}, and {{eye_color}} eyes.  
Wears {{clothing}}, featuring {{symbols_or_details}}.  
Carries {{weapons_or_equipment}}, {{visible_features like scars, tattoos}}.  
Expression is {{expression}}.  
Clothing and skin show signs of {{wear_signs like grime, blood, scratches}}.

**Negative prompt:**
anime, cartoon, photorealistic, CGI, 3D render, digital art, overexposed, modern clothing, soft lighting, glossy, bokeh, bright colors, saturated, fantasy armor, sci-fi gear, cyberpunk, neon, futuristic tech, symmetrical face, smiling, perfect skin, makeup



---

**Example:**
Subject is a 45-year-old woman with sharp cheekbones, white short-cropped hair, and grey eyes.  
Wears a formal but worn red officer’s coat with gold trim, a high collar, and a cracked Aquila medallion.  
Hands rest on a command table. A long scar crosses her left eyebrow.  
Expression is steely and unyielding. Her face and gloves are stained with grime.


# 🏞️ Landscape Prompt Template

**Base prompt:**
A traditional oil painting in the grimdark Warhammer 40,000 style, depicting a bleak and atmospheric landscape. Rendered with heavy, textured brushwork and a muted, desaturated color palette. The scene is bathed in harsh, directional light, with deep shadows and overcast skies. Everything feels aged, decaying, and burdened by time. The architecture is monolithic and brutalist, often cracked or overgrown. No fantasy or sci-fi elements should be explicit – everything grounded in a 40k imperial setting.

**Template:**
The landscape shows {{landscape_type}}, with {{dominant_features}}.  
The atmosphere is {{adjectives like oppressive, windswept, choking}}.  
Human figures are {{optional_description}}.  
The entire scene evokes {{emotions like despair, vigilance, dread}}.

**Negative promopt:**
photorealistic, modern buildings, skyscrapers, clean architecture, glass facades, cyberpunk, neon, futuristic vehicles, soft lighting, colorful, lush nature, idyllic, magical, fantasy ruins, happy mood, optimistic




---

**Example:**
The landscape shows a decaying feudal citadel built into the cliffs of a wind-scarred ridge, its spires cracked and darkened by centuries of acid rain.  
Worn banners flap from broken towers. Below, a procession of cowled serfs carries relics across a mud-choked causeway.  
The atmosphere is thick with soot and fog.  
A feeling of cold inevitability permeates the air.
